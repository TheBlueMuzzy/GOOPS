# Changelog

## v0.2.0 — 2026-10-29

The whole career is here: every rank from 0 to 50, the story of what the goop really is, the final shift, and the ending. Upgrades and active abilities open as you rank up, and you can win the game and begin a new career.

### New
- The story, one moment at every promotion: Management on the monitor, the Veteran on the speaker, and later a third voice no one warned you about.
- Sign your contract on your first day. Your name is saved on your device, and someone will use it later.
- Payday after every promotion: your pay, your fines and what you still owe for your passage, line by line.
- Upgrades from rank 5: spend scraps on better tools at the console and take them back for free. New ones keep opening up to rank 38.
- Active abilities to equip and fire mid-shift: Cooldown Booster, Goop Dump, Goop Colorizer and Crack Down, with up to three slots (keys 1, 2, 3 or the charge buttons).
- New twists as the ranks climb: wild goop that matches any crack, two-colour pieces and cracks that grow.
- Faces in the goop. Look closely.
- The final shift at rank 50: an all-black tank, an onslaught of cracks, help from someone you didn't expect, and a valve to open three times.
- The ending, and the end-game screen. After it you can begin a new career with a gold-trimmed console.
- A new zap: the laser beam heats the goop white-hot and sprays droplets.
- For testers: Rank Select on the console starts any rank from 0 to 50. Rank 0 starts over as a brand-new device.

### Changed
- The first day has more of the original's touches: poking goop that can't pop makes it jiggle, and a message explains when a crack is covered by the wrong colour.
- The Veteran's speech bubble is easier to read, and payday lines appear one at a time.
- Headings use the same pixel font as the rest of the game, and the browser tab shows the game's icon.

### Known issues
- The sounds are still placeholders. Final sound and music are planned for 1.0.
- A few drawings are placeholders, including the hatch under the console.
- No Credits screen yet. It's planned for 1.0.
- With Text size at 125% or more, the console's words crowd together and the Upgrades button partly covers the Settings gear. Fix coming; workaround: tap the edge of the gear.
- Balance from rank 10 up is a first pass; beta players' runs will tune it.
- On a busy device an update can take one extra visit to arrive. Workaround: reopen the game.
- Opening the old Goops (thebluemuzzy.github.io/GOOPS/) can remove this version's offline copy until its next update; it still works online.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-20

Goops is rebuilt from the ground up. This alpha is the whole core job: a guided first day, then shifts in the goop tank up to rank 9, with three machines that break and need fixing, and sound.

### New
- A guided first day teaches the game step by step, from pulling the periscope to your first promotion. It can be replayed from Settings.
- Shifts: pressure rises and cracks form in the tank wall. Cover a crack with goop of the same colour, then zap that goop to seal it. Seal enough cracks to meet the quota before the tank ruptures.
- Soft, squishy goop: pieces of the same colour join into one blob, and a blob has to fill up before it can be zapped.
- The console between shifts: pull the periscope down to start a shift, come back up to rest. Score turns into XP and ranks.
- The laser, the visor and the controls break during shifts and are fixed at the console. The first time each one breaks, the game shows how to fix it.
- Promotions unlock the next-piece window (rank 1), swapping with the next piece (rank 3) and the Upgrades button (rank 5).
- Cracks are easy to read: bubbles rise from open cracks, an x-ray view shows them through goop, a red X marks a crack covered by the wrong colour, and sealed cracks leave a scar.
- Phone controls where every finger does its own thing, with a swap ring next to the finger holding it. Keyboard and mouse work too.
- Sound effects, with volume in Settings.
- Installs as a full-screen app from the browser, plays offline and updates itself. The version is shown on the console and in Settings → About.
- Testers can open a tuning panel (the ` key, or a triple-tap) to try settings and copy a bug report.

### Known issues
- Upgrades aren't in yet — the Upgrades button says they arrive in a later update. No story yet.
- The sounds are placeholders.
- Portrait only: turning the phone sideways pauses the game until it's upright again.
- Opening the old Goops (thebluemuzzy.github.io/GOOPS/) after installing the alpha can remove the alpha's offline copy until its next update; it still works online.
