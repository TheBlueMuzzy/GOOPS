Goops — the economy sim

Bots play whole careers — rank 0 to the final shift — on the real game and the real tuning files, earning XP and scraps and buying upgrades three different ways. 72 careers (8 per bot × buy style), 14368 shifts in all, 757.1 s. Made 2026-10-29T14:07:01.702Z. Same seeds = the same numbers.

A shift is won when its crack quota (the cracks you must seal) is met; a lost shift still earns XP. A stall = the same rank lost 3 or more shifts in a row. Scraps = 1 per rank, spent on upgrade levels. Bots: Casual ignores cracks, Focused chases them, Sharp also vents pressure and keeps the stack low. They go to the console to repair, swap with a hold, fire their actives, and shop between shifts.

"Noticeable" uses a placeholder line until Muzzy calibrates it: 5 s longer before the tank breaks, or 5% more shifts won, or 5% more of the quota sealed — and bigger than twice the seed-to-seed wobble (any change reshuffles the whole shift, so small differences are just luck). The best bot × rank is shown; hover for the seeds that changed at all. "Tank lasts" is counted on the shift's own clock (time at the console runs slower there), so a quicker repair doesn't look like a shorter-lived tank.
