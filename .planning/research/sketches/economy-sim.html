Goops — the economy sim

Bots play whole careers — rank 0 to the final shift — on the real game and the real tuning files, earning XP and scraps and buying upgrades three different ways. 72 careers (8 per bot × buy style), 33283 shifts in all, 1556.8 s. Made 2026-10-30T14:26:53.189Z. Same seeds = the same numbers.

A shift is won when its crack quota (the cracks you must seal) is met; a lost shift still earns XP. A stall = the same rank lost 3 or more shifts in a row. Scraps = 1 per rank, spent on upgrade levels. Bots: Casual ignores cracks, Focused chases them, Sharp also vents pressure and keeps the stack low. They go to the console to repair, swap with a hold, fire their actives, and shop between shifts.

"Tank lasts" is counted on the shift's own clock (time at the console runs slower there), so a quicker repair doesn't look like a shorter-lived tank.
